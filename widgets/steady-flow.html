Steady-flow devices and the SFEE

Air flows steadily through a device. Change the inlet and what the device does, and watch the energy balance: heat and work in (left of the SFEE) always equal the change in enthalpy and kinetic energy of the flow (right). Air is an ideal gas (R = 287 J/(kg K), cp = 1005 J/(kg K)), so its density is ρ = P/(RT) and he − hi = cp(Te − Ti). The velocities follow from conservation of mass, ṁ = ρAC, with the duct areas fixed (except where stated); changes in height are neglected.
